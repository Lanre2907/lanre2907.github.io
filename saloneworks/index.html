# The job discovery of HiringCafe, the recruitment marketplace of LinkedIn, and the hiring tools of modern applicant-tracking systems, designed for African markets.

For job seekers

Free job discovery. Search and filter by sector, job type, salary and location, then apply in one place.

For employers

Post jobs directly, search for qualified talent, manage applications and streamline hiring from one platform.

Roadmap: AI

Discover and organize opportunities, remove duplicate and outdated listings, and match candidates by skills, qualifications and experience.
